## 1.0.0

- **First release:** bars of inventory items anywhere on screen; click one or press its hotkey to use it.
- **Layouts:** sets of bars with their own item hotkeys. Switch by hotkey or menu, or automatically for each character or profession.
- **Deposit All Materials** from a bar or a hotkey.
- **Edit on screen:** drag bars and items, and right-click for columns, icon size, hotkeys and layouts.
- **Large bags:** scrolls the inventory to find items that aren't in view, then scrolls it back.
- **Choose from your bags** with an API key, or paste any item's chat link.
- **Colour matching** tells apart items that share a picture in different colours.
- **Off in PvP and WvW:** bars hidden, hotkeys off.

# Quick Item

Put bars of your inventory items anywhere on screen: food, utilities, boosters, gizmos, anything you double-click in your bags. Click one, or press its hotkey, and Quick Item opens your inventory, finds the item, uses it and closes the inventory again. Your cursor goes back where it was.

**Off in PvP and WvW:** in structured PvP and World vs. World, lobbies included, the bars are hidden, the hotkeys let every key through to the game, and nothing is used. Its icon is greyed out meanwhile.

## How to Use

1. Right-click the Quick Item icon at the top left to edit the bars. The first time, an empty bar is already waiting in the middle of the screen.
2. Click **+** and choose an item, or **Deposit All Materials** at the top of the list. With an API key, the items in your bags are listed; otherwise paste an item's chat link.
3. Drag the bar's header to move it, and drag items to rearrange them. Right-click for columns, icon size, hotkeys and more.
4. Right-click the icon again to lock the bars, then double-click an item to use it.

The item must be in this character's bags, and chat must be closed. If it isn't in view, Quick Item scrolls the inventory to find it, then scrolls back. It finds the inventory window by the bag picture at its top left, so keep that on screen.

## Features

- **Any number of bars,** each with its own size and columns, and optionally shown on one character only.
- **Layouts:** keep sets of bars for different jobs and switch with a hotkey or a menu, or let them switch by themselves for each character or profession. Each layout has its own item hotkeys, and a bar can be in every layout.
- **Deposit All Materials:** press the inventory's button from a bar or a hotkey, without opening the inventory yourself.
- **Hotkeys:** give any item its own key; it works even with the bars hidden.
- **Large bags:** items out of view are found by scrolling the inventory.
- **Finds the right one:** icons are matched in colour, so items that share a picture in different colours aren't mixed up.
- **Stays out of the way:** hide the bars in combat or with the map open, or toggle them with a hotkey.
- **Stops safely:** if you alt-tab, open chat or hit a loading screen partway through, it stops and tells you why.

## Settings

Left-click the Quick Item icon to open the settings, or press **Settings** on the module's page in Blish HUD. Each slider has a box to type its value in and a **Default** button. They're grouped into Corner Icon, Bars, Layouts and Using Items. The [full settings list](https://github.com/mfdavies7/BlishHud-Modules/blob/main/QuickItem/README.md#settings) describes each one.

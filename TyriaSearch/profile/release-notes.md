- **Map markers show their distance** (minimap optional).
- **Settings:** Default buttons for sliders; the icon's tooltip shows your hotkey.
- **New icons,** lit on hover, keeping their place in Bag of Holding.
- **Fixes:** flickering icons, failed icon downloads, a stuck tooltip.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **One search box for Tyria:** waypoints, landmarks, hearts, crafting stations, NPCs, events, portals, effects, items, skills, traits and more.
- **Search any way:** by name, map, chat code, NPC service ("bank"), or two maps for the portals between them.
- **Act on a result:** copy, paste or whisper its chat link, mark it on the map, or open its wiki page.
- **Wiki results** below your own, and game tooltips for skills, traits and items.
- **Map markers** on the world map and minimap that point the way when off screen.
- **Portals on the world map,** labelled with where they lead. New ones you take can be added with Learn New Portals.
- **Favourites** and a **Second Search Language**.
- **Keeps itself up to date** after the first download.

## 1.0.1

- **Better ordering:** exact names first, then names starting with what you typed. Within each, results on your map come first, then the nearest.
- **No duplicates:** identical copies of an item are listed once, showing your favourite if one is.
- **Find NPCs by what's beside them:** "cooking station", "crafting station", "vault", "TP", "repair anvil", "forge".
- **Healing and barrier in tooltips** show the amount and Healing Power coefficient.
- **Learn New Portals is now off by default,** and can ignore a whole destination. Fractals, guild halls, homesteads, PvP and WvW are never asked about.
- **Fixes:** flickering coin and marker icons, icons that failed to download, a stuck download tooltip.
- **Map markers show how far away they are** (minimap optional).
- **New icons.**

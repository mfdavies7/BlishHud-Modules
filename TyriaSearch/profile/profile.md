# Tyria Search

One search box for everything in Tyria: waypoints, points of interest, vistas, renown hearts, hero challenges, mastery points, adventures, NPCs, events, portals, effects, items, skills and traits. Find what you're after by name, map or chat code, then copy, paste or whisper its chat link, or mark it on the map.

## How to Use

Right-click the magnifier icon at the top left, or press a hotkey, and start typing. Results update as you type. Pick a category from the dropdown, or keep it on Everything. With the search box empty, you see your favourites.

The first time, the search data is downloaded, which takes a few minutes. You can search what has arrived while the rest downloads.

- **Search by map or area:** "Queensdale" lists its landmarks; "Queensdale hero challenge" finds that map's hero challenges.
- **Search by chat code:** paste a code to see what it is.
- **Find NPCs by service:** "bank" lists every banker, each with the waypoint nearest to them. "Cooking station" or "crafting station" finds the master craftsmen who stand at them.
- **Find portals between maps:** "Black Citadel to Lion's Arch", "gate from Hoelbrak".
- **A second language:** names show in Blish HUD's language, and you can also match names in a second one.
- **The wiki:** pages from the wiki are listed below the results as you type.

Each result shows what it is and where: "Waypoint · Shaemoor Fields · Queensdale". Hover over a skill, trait, item or effect to see its game tooltip.

## Actions

- **Click** a result to copy its chat link. For NPCs, events and other things without a link of their own, that's the nearest waypoint.
- **Enter** copies the top result and returns you to the game, ready to paste.
- **Right-click** for everything else: paste into chat, whisper to yourself, show on the map, copy a /wiki command, open the wiki page, or add to favourites.

What clicking and right-clicking do can be changed in the settings.

## The Map

- **Show on Map** marks results on the world map and minimap, as many as you like. A marker that's off screen sits at the edge, pointing the way, and each shows how far away it is. Right-click a marker to remove it.
- **Portals on the map:** optionally mark every zone portal, asura gate, and dungeon, raid and fractal entrance, labelled with where they lead.
- **Learns new portals:** turn on Learn New Portals, take a portal it doesn't know, and it offers to add it, then asks you to go back through for the return portal.

## Settings

Left-click the Tyria Search icon to open the settings, or press **Settings** on the module's page in Blish HUD. They're grouped into Search Window, Search Options (which kinds of result to include), Search Data (second language, how often to download), World Map, Portals and Chat. The [full settings list](https://github.com/mfdavies7/BlishHud-Modules/blob/main/TyriaSearch/README.md#settings) describes each one.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Shift + left-click to open the wiki:** works on items in your inventory, bank and the Trading Post, rewards, skills and points on the world map.
- **Wiki in your language:** follows the game's language, or pick English, German, French or Spanish.
- **Easy to turn off:** use the Toggle Hotkey or right-click the icon to shift-click into chat as usual. The icon greys out while it's off.
- **Hint while holding Shift** shows that a click will search.
- **If the command is typed wrong:** raise Key Press Delay, or turn on Wait for Shift Release Before Sending.

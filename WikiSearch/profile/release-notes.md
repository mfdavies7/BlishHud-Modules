## 1.0.1

- **Tidier Shift hint:** smaller, matching the game's tooltips, with its text wrapped.
- **Hide Hint Over Blish HUD Windows:** a new setting (on by default). The hint always hides over corner icons.
- **Turn Off on Right-Click:** a new setting (off by default) that turns Wiki Search off when you right-click in the game.
- **The icon's tooltip** shows its name and your Toggle Hotkey.
- **Default buttons** for the sliders in the settings.
- **New icons** that light up on hover like Blish HUD's own, and keep their place in Bag of Holding between sessions.
